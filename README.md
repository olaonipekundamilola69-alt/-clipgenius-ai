ClipGenius AI ✂️

Turn long videos into engaging short clips.

ClipGenius AI is an AI-powered video clipping platform designed to help content creators find interesting moments in long videos and turn them into short-form content.

Planned Features

* AI-powered highlight detection
* Automatic captions and subtitles
* Smart vertical video formatting
* Custom clip lengths
* Video preview and downloads
* Support for gaming, podcasts, interviews and more

Project Status

🚧 Currently under development.

Our goal is to make video editing and clipping easier for creators.
